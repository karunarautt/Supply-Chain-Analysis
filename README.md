# Supply Chain Analysis

## Project Overview

Analyzed a 100-record supply chain dataset to evaluate product performance, revenue, inventory levels, supplier performance, and transportation metrics using MySQL and Power BI.

## Objectives

- Analyze revenue and product sales performance
- Compare performance across product types
- Evaluate supplier revenue contribution
- Analyze transportation modes and revenue share
- Examine the relationship between product price and products sold
- Review inventory and defect-rate metrics

## Tools & Technologies

- MySQL
- SQL
- Power BI
- Excel
- CSV

## Key Metrics

| Metric | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Records | 100 |
| Total Revenue | 577,604.86 |
| Products Sold | 46,099 |
| Average Stock Level | 47.77 |
| Average Defect Rate | 2.28 |
| Average Shipping Cost | 5.55 |

## Power BI Dashboard

The interactive dashboard provides an overview of supply chain performance, including:

- Revenue by Product Type
- Products Sold by Product Type
- Revenue by Supplier

- Price vs. Products Sold
- Product Type filtering

## SQL Analysis

SQL was used to perform analysis such as:

- Revenue analysis by product type
- Products sold by product type
- Revenue by supplier
- Revenue by transportation mode
- Average shipping cost by transportation mode
- Average production volume by product type
- Overall supply chain summary

## Key Insights

- Skincare generated the highest revenue among the product types.
- Skincare also had the highest number of products sold.
- Supplier 1 generated the highest revenue among the suppliers analyzed.
- Rail transportation generated the highest revenue share among the transportation modes.
- The overall average stock level was 47.77.
- The overall average defect rate was 2.28.
